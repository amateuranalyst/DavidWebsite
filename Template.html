ARR — Annual Recurring Revenue
Total committed, recognised revenue on an annual basis. Includes renewals. Excludes one-time professional services fees.
Quota Attainment
🟢 ≥85% · 🟡 70–84% · 🔴 <70% — reviewed quarterly with Revenue Ops
Closed-won value ÷ assigned quota target × 100. Reported at rep, region, and BU level.
Pipeline Coverage
🟢 ≥3× · 🟡 2–2.9× · 🔴 <2×
Open pipeline value ÷ remaining quota target. Calculated on opportunities in active stages only (excludes Verbal/Closed).
Gross Retention Rate
🟢 ≥90% · 🟡 80–89% · 🔴 <80%
Revenue retained from existing customers excluding expansions. Calculated at end of each renewal period from NetSuite contract data.
Alternate State (Period Comparison)
The Trends and Scatter sheets support Qlik Alternate States. The toggle labelled "Prior Year" activates the comparison state — bars turn Packard Blue for the alternate period.
FX Treatment
All currencies converted using month-end rates from the Finance data warehouse. Real-time FX is not applied. APAC figures may differ from local reporting by up to 24h.
1
Navigate between sheets
Use the top navigation bar. Analytics sheets are grouped under the "Analytics" dropdown — hover or click to expand and select a specific analysis view.
2
Apply and save filters
Filters are available on all Analytics sheets (Sheets 02–06). Click any chip to open a dropdown selection. Hit "Save Bookmark" to preserve your current filter state for future visits.
3
Period comparison (Alternate States)
On Trends and Scatter sheets, activate the "Prior Year" toggle to overlay a second time period. This uses Qlik Alternate States — the comparison series shows in Packard Blue.
4
Export a report
Go to Sheet 07 (Reports). Apply your filters first, then click the export button for your desired format. The export captures your current selection state.
5
Something looks wrong?
Contact the BI team at bi@versigent.com. Include a screenshot and the current URL (which encodes your active selections). Do not clear your selections before reaching out.
